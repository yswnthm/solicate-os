## What Was Completed

1. **Index Cleanup & Staging Elimination**:
   - 20 staging/duplicate pages permanently removed from index (`/reserve-old`, `/sacred-offerings-old`, `/z-home-old`, `/shop-old-feb`, etc.).
   - 17 utility and transactional pages set to `noindex` (`/cart/`, `/checkout/`, `/my-account/`, `/membership/`, etc.) keeping Google focused purely on revenue-generating pages.
   - 301 permanent redirect map built for legacy URLs.

2. **On-Page SEO Matrix**:
   - Optimized Title tags, Meta descriptions, and single H1 headings for all 14 canonical pages.
   - Internal linking anchors mapped to support upcoming content pillars.
   - Descriptive image alt-text standards defined for somatic and water sound bath media.

3. **Structured Data (Schema.org JSON-LD)**:
   - Implemented `HealthAndBeautyBusiness` schema with exact Bowen Island coordinates (49.38°N, 123.33°W).
   - Dynamic `Event` schema with `eventStatus` and ticket `offers` for automated rich snippets in Google search results.
   - Founder E-E-A-T credentials attached to Organization graph.

4. **AI Search Engine Ready (`/llms.txt`)**:
   - Structured markdown knowledge file created at `/llms.txt` for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.

5. **Analytics & Google Business Profile**:
   - GA4 key conversion events configured (`purchase`, `begin_checkout`, `join_waitlist`, `corporate_inquiry`).
   - Google Business Profile copy, categories (*Meditation Center* / *Retreat Center*), and review generation link prepared.

6. **90-Day Content & Growth Roadmap**:
   - 12 high-intent topics planned across 4 core pillars (Floating Sound Baths, Somatic Nervous Regulation, Men's Grounding, Family Somatics).
